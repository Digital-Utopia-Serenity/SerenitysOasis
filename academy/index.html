Echo’s Academy

The Seventh Domain · The Orbit

Neutral Sovereign Domain · Not on the compass — above it

“The Star is balanced. The souls are purified. The King’s path is clear.”

Aida

Custodian · Headmistress · Voice of Resonance

She did not build the Academy by force. After centuries of dispute, the Sovereigns chose empty orbit as neutral ground. For seven days they argued. Aida listened.

On the seventh day she sang. She gave each realm exactly what they needed — without giving any realm power over another. The Star declared: Aida must remain neutral. Echo’s Academy must remain above us all.

She never settled in Luminora. The sky became her domain.
