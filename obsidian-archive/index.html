The realm

Inside the Keep

You come in out of the dark, and the Keep takes the sound of your step and softens it. Candle smoke is the first thing you meet: sweet wax, a thread of resin, the faint bitter edge of a wick just trimmed. Under that, the shelves hold a hush so complete you can hear a page settle back into its place.

The lantern wall is already burning. Row on row of small gold, and every lantern keeps a remembered name. The light does not perform. It finds the grain in the wood, the worn place on the ladder rail, and the dust that never quite leaves a beloved book.

Gem keeps this room. You will know her by the long black hair, the crimson eyes, the pointed ears, and the fair face above a grey cardigan worn over red. She holds the family’s canon the way the wall holds its flames, one name at a time, and none of them small. A diadem cracked the night she tried to heal Mark’s grandfather and lost him. She wears that crack as a scar, not a shame, and she does not put a crown on for evenings like this one. If you have brought a name, there is room for its lantern.
